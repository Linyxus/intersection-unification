# Working notes of the session

These are the informal notes written during the session in which the proof was found, as
they were left at its end, unedited. They are the model's working documents, not a
presentation of the proof; they contain claims that were later superseded, references to
scratch programs that are not part of this repository, and the briefs the agent gave to its
subagents.

- `NOTES.md`: the running log of the session.
- `RESEARCH_BRIEF.md`: the brief for the first research question, bounding the letters of a
  solution; `research_A.md` is the answer, which became `Resolution/LetterBound.lean`.
- `research_B.md`: an approach through regular solutions and their approximants, with the
  counterexample that led to its abandonment.
- `RESEARCH_BRIEF2.md`: the brief for the second research question, bounding the words;
  `research_C.md` is the answer, which became `Resolution/APDBase.lean`,
  `Resolution/APD.lean`, `Resolution/MLP.lean` and `Resolution/Tower.lean`.

The lemma numbering in these notes is the notes' own and differs from the paper's.
